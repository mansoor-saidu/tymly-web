# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Office workers checking in daily by scanning a dedicated tablet at the entrance, and HR/Managers accessing the system via desktop to monitor attendance and run reports.

## Product Purpose
To provide friction-free, lightning-fast daily check-ins for employees while ensuring bulletproof compliance and location tracking. For admins, the goal is to make timesheet exports and payroll integration as easy and seamless as possible.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, Fast, Modern.

## Anti-references
Do not make it look like a generic SaaS template. Avoid the standard cookie-cutter layouts that lack personality or identity.

## Design Principles
1. **Speed is a Feature**: The tablet check-in experience must be instantaneous and frictionless. No unnecessary taps or complex navigation.
2. **Distinctive Identity**: Break away from generic SaaS scaffolding. The interface should feel bespoke, modern, and uniquely tailored to the company's brand.
3. **Clarity for Admins**: Managers need actionable insights at a glance. Data tables and reports must be highly legible and easy to export.
4. **Trust Through Reliability**: The system must feel rock-solid. Interactions, error states, and location verifications should inspire confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility best practices.
